The Convergence: Geotechnics, Tennis & Tai Chi

Geotechnics investigates how structures anchor into the earth and transfer colossal forces through soil and rock. Tennis harnesses the ground reaction force through the kinetic chain to propel a ball at high velocity. Tai Chi trains the body to root into the earth, sink mass, and transmit effortless force through fascial elasticity without localized tension. All three disciplines are fundamentally about how force is grounded, organized, and balanced from the foundation up.
